1. About cookies

In accordance with Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), websites using non-essential cookies are required to obtain the user's express consent.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are web server tools used to store and retrieve information about visitors. They allow servers to remember user preferences, login credentials and browsing interests. Similar technologies include local shared objects, Flash cookies and pixels.

3. Cookies subject to regulation and exempt cookies

Under European directives, analytics and advertising cookies require informed consent. Technical cookies and those necessary for the operation of the website or explicitly requested services are exempt.

4. Types of cookies by purpose

Technical and functional cookies

Enable browsing the website and using its services.

Analytics cookies

Track user behaviour and measure site activity to improve the service.

Advertising cookies

Manage ad spaces based on content and frequency criteria.

Behavioural advertising cookies

Collect user preferences for retargeting purposes.

Social cookies

Enable content sharing through social platforms; those platforms may track activity outside the service.

Affiliate cookies

Monitor visits from partner websites.

Security cookies

Store encrypted data to defend against malicious attacks.

5. By ownership

First-party cookies

Sent from domains managed by the website owner.

Third-party cookies

Sent from external domains that manage their own data collection.

6. By retention period

Session cookies

Collect data only during active browsing sessions.

Persistent cookies

Remain stored for defined periods set by the cookie operators.

7. Accept, reject or delete cookies

The website prominently displays cookie information. Users can accept all cookies through an affirmative action or reject them entirely. Rejection may limit functionality. Previously installed cookies can be deleted through browser settings.

8. Change your cookie settings

Users can restrict, block or delete cookies through their browser settings. You can find configuration instructions for the following browsers:

  • Internet Explorer
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Firefox
  • Chrome
  • Safari

11. Personal data processing

The company acts as data controller in accordance with the GDPR. Processing purposes align with each cookie's declared function. The legal basis derives from necessity or user consent. Data is retained according to each cookie's specifications. Data is shared with third parties only in the case of third-party cookies or legal obligations.

User rights:

  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Access, correct, port or delete data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)
