FAQs

Everything you need to know about Brand Radar.

Which channels does Brand Radar monitor?

Brand Radar covers digital media and press, social networks (X, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok), search engines and — uniquely — the most relevant generative AI models: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and other LLMs. It's the only solution that closes the full brand perception loop.

How often is the data refreshed?

Media and social data refresh in under 24 hours. AI visibility monitoring runs on a configurable cadence. You get automatic alerts whenever meaningful changes are detected — no need to log into the dashboard.

How is it different from tools like Brandwatch or Mention?

Traditional social-listening tools don't measure how AI sees you — and that's where a large share of new-consumer opinion is now formed. Brand Radar integrates generative-AI monitoring alongside traditional listening and adds continuous competitive benchmarking across every channel.

What do I get as a client? Is it only technology or is there a team behind it?

Brand Radar combines proprietary technology with a senior analysis team. You don't just get data: you get interpretation, strategic context and actionable recommendations. Every month there's a review session with the T&T team to prioritize responses and adjust strategy.

How long until it's operational?

Standard onboarding takes 5 working days. It includes configuring keywords, competitors and relevant sources for your sector, integration with your owned channels where applicable, and a system calibration session with the team.
